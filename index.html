Founder & Head Baker

Meet the baker

The hands behind every box

Every order that leaves this kitchen is mixed, shaped and baked by one person — not a production line. She believes she has always had a natural connection with food, and the joy and purpose she finds in creating and feeding people with love is unmatched. That's why nothing goes out that she wouldn't serve her own family.

  • 9+ years of baking 5+ years of professional industry experience, with a Diploma in Bakery & Patisserie from IHM and professional training at Theos, Noida.
  • 500+ customers served Years of baking, creating and sharing food with 500+ customers along the way.
  • From passion to profession Self-taught from childhood, professionally trained, and previously co-founded a cloud-kitchen bakery with her best friend.
  • From Lucknow to Europe Conducted food workshops in Helsinki, Finland, including a three-course Indian meal for locals.
"I grew up watching the women in my family make food with so much love — my mother, my grandmother and my maternal grandmother. Sura is inspired by them, and is my way of carrying that same warmth forward, one box at a time."

The story behind Sura

Sura is a legacy project inspired by three generations of women in her family. The initials of her mother, grandmother and maternal grandmother come together to form the name Sura — making it a little piece of her family's legacy.

Made with the finest ingredients

Only pure, quality ingredients go into our bakes — no artificial flavourings or preservatives. We use couverture chocolate, real butter, fresh dairy and carefully sourced ingredients wherever possible.
